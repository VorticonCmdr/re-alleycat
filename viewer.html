THE PC SPEAKER

Small speaker. Big personality.

The title melody is stored as note indices and PIT divisors, not a recorded audio file. Listen to a square-wave reconstruction at the original BIOS tick rate.

Other effects are generated by game code, not standalone sound assets. Their bytes remain accessible in the data explorer. Title playback is available for the verified IBM executable (PC speaker) and the verified Tandy executable (three SN76496 voices).
